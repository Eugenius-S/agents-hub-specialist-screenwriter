# Where to take more from

The licenses of the open repos were checked; do not copy finished texts, take devices. The links are pinned to a commit: before using anything newer in a repo, read it and check its license again.

## Open repositories

Licenses checked through the GitHub API. I read only descriptions and excerpts, not whole code. Do not copy finished texts, take devices.
1. github.com/Creatify-AI/video-ad-generator/tree/d35e249fd956d600731cb6b23499ebe2adfbe7c5, MIT. Hook formulas (pattern interrupt, a question, a bold claim, POV, a number), body structures (PAS, a cascade of functions, a stack of proof, before/after, a day in the life) with timings for 15 and 30 s, a matrix of formats 9:16 / 16:9. The Creatify API is not needed.
2. github.com/promptmetrics/video-skill/tree/544723f3c185ffa67d158958eb1d0fee128a1781, MIT. The chain: requirements, a script (a human approves it), a frame-by-frame storyboard, a design specification. The idea "the script is approved before the storyboard" is useful for our process.
3. github.com/iart-ai/explainer-video-skills/tree/3e2d411b725d9a72939cf8e5eb81579e751373e7, MIT, and github.com/runesleo/claude-video-kit/tree/c04af591bc2ced8764b63b10c7de2d121661f37e, MIT. A script and storyboard for voiced explainers. By description only, I did not look inside.
4. github.com/criscatalyst/creator-skills/tree/47dcc85cc1d8132b498ef1e451d270d95c06d6c5, MIT (the new address of script-writer; the old criscatalyst/script-writer-skill I could not open through the API). By the old repo's README: Hook, Build-Up, Value, Payoff, CTA, 100+ hooks, durations 15 / 30 / 60 / 90 s. I did not check the new repo's contents.
5. github.com/xnomad-dev/claude-skills/tree/3ed47d680161d9e21adad36b4acbef090e637e7b (`video-script-writer/SKILL.md`): no license is stated, so copying is not allowed. The idea: the rules "do not start with a greeting", the labels [B-ROLL], [TEXT ON SCREEN], a list of shots.
6. github.com/zhuyansen/awesome-claude-video-skills/tree/b4b7c042f0b95f875a93c8401991e6a25eb11916: a catalog of about 180 repositories for finding new ones; check the licenses of individual repositories every time.
