# Methods and formats of videos

Tags: [off] an official source of the platform, [sec] secondary (blog, agency), [loc] a local file, [own] our guideline. Google and TikTok data are correlational: what the best videos do, not the cause.

## Methods

1. A hook in the first 3 s. The main message or the product in frame at once. TikTok: a hook in the first 3-6 s, 90% of the effect on ad recall falls on the first 6 s. Meta: the brand and the message in the first 3 s. [off] ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/creative-best-practices-top-performing-ads; business.facebook.com/business/learn/video/reels-best-practices
2. Google ABCD (Attract, Brand, Connect, Direct): more than two shots in the first 5 s, the brand in the first 5 s, a person in frame, a call by voice and not only by caption. Videos that meet more criteria more often drive sales growth (Google/Kantar: +30% probability of short-term sales growth, +17% brand contribution). [off] services.google.com/fh/files/misc/youtube_google_abcd_reference_guide_en.pdf; support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551
3. Open with a benefit, not a lead-in. "Today I want to tell you" and "Hi, friends" eat the hook window. The hook is written first and holds the promise for the whole video. [off] tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/build-a-repeatable-format (Hook, Hold, Act)
4. Hold: after the hook something changes on screen every 2-4 s (a zoom, a cut, a caption, a sound). One shot for 30 s does not hold even with strong text. [off] same place
5. Act: one concrete call, not "like and subscribe". The ask comes after the benefit is shown. [off] same place; [off] ABCD ("Direct")
6. One idea per video. The message fits in 25 words; if you need "and" twice, it is two videos. [sec] animationexplainers.com/how-to-write-a-video-script/
7. Show, do not tell: the action on screen matches the word, the camera follows the words. The picture illustrates the spoken line and does not duplicate it. [loc] the editor's principles; explainer-video
8. Problem, agitation, solution (PAS): name the pain, bring it to "this is about me", show the product as the way out. In 15 s the agitation shrinks to one frame. [sec] copyhackers.com/2015/10/copywriting-formula; [sec] github.com/Creatify-AI/video-ad-generator (PAS timings for 15 and 30 s)
9. Hook types to choose three variants from for every video: pain ("tried everything, nothing worked until..."), curiosity, social proof, the result first, an unexpected frame. Test: the same body, another hook. [sec] trackbee.io (blog on Meta/TikTok UGC); agrowth.io (blog on TikTok hooks)
10. Duration by platform. TikTok: videos of 9-15 s work better. Meta: Feed 6-15 s, Stories up to 10 s. UGC videos: Reels 15-30 s, TikTok 15-45 s. Guides by block (hook / body / call, s): Meta 1-3 / 7-20 / 2-5; TikTok 1-2 / 6-18 / 2-4. These are agency guides, not laws. [off] TikTok, Meta; [sec] sovran.ai/blog/hook-body-cta-video-ad-structure
11. Sound is on: 95% of YouTube views are with sound [off ABCD]; TikTok: 88% say sound is important [off]. But Meta advises building a video also for muted sound with captions: the meaning must read without the voice. [off]
12. Voice-over pace, English: about 150 words per minute (2.5 words/s): 15 s is about 35 words, 30 s about 75, 60 s about 150. Boords counts at 130 wpm. [sec] animationexplainers.com; assets.boords.com/video-script-template; thevoicerealm.com/blog/voice-over-words-per-minute-script-timing
13. Voice-over pace, Russian: radio ads 115-150 words per minute: image about 115, broadcast about 135, dense about 150, that is a 30 s spot fits 56 / 68 / 79 words. The average announcer delivery is about 130 words/min. Hronomer Pro counts by syllables and writes out numbers in words. [sec] hronomer.m10m.ru; sfmedia.ru/calculate/readingtime. For us: 2.2-2.3 words/s, as in explainer-video.
14. Numbers and addresses take longer to read than they look: "$25,990" is several words on air, a phone number about 3 s. Keep digits to a minimum in the voice-over, move the number to a caption. [sec] hronomer.m10m.ru
15. Write voice-over for the ear: short sentences, read aloud, cut what you would not say to a colleague. 300 words in a 30-second video make a tongue twister: cut ideas, do not speed up the voice. [sec] animationexplainers.com; swarmify.com/blog/script-writing/
16. Subtitles, safe zone, loudness, the end card: the editor's rules, not duplicated here. [loc]

Not verified: the primary sources of speech rate (NCVS, Brysbaert 2019) came up in retellings in search, I did not open the pages. "The hook gives 70% of UGC success" (trackbee) is a blog claim without research, do not use it as a rule.

## Formats

Words of voice-over: seconds x 2.3, minus pauses and the end card. A scene = words / 2.3 + 0.4 s. A beat = one idea on screen, a new reason to watch every 5-7 s.

1. An ad of 15 s (Reels, TikTok, Shorts), <= 30 words.
   - 0-3 hook: pain or result, the product in frame, a bright first frame, no logo intro.
   - 3-8 a demo of the key action, 2-3 shots.
   - 8-12 the result, "before and after" ahead of the explanation.
   - 12-15 the end card + one call phrase, hold for 2-3 s.
2. An ad of 30 s, <= 60 words.
   - 0-3 hook. 3-8 the problem (PAS, agitation in one phrase).
   - 8-22 the solution and demo, 3 steps of 4-5 s. 22-26 the result or proof. 26-30 the end card and the call.
3. A product demo of 20-35 s (a design-tool plugin, an MCP, a site).
   - 0-3 the product is open at once (a click on the launch icon only in the ending), a promise of the result in the voice.
   - 3-12 the main action, the screen 1:1 from the design tool, the camera follows the words.
   - 12-20 the result, then "by the way" with a side function. 20-30 the site, scroll only down, the end card 2-3 s. [loc] the editor's principles
4. An explainer of 60-90 s, 138 / 207 words. The arc 20 / 15 / 45 / 20 %.
   - 60 s: the problem 0-12, the solution 12-21, how it works 21-48 (three steps of about 9 s), the result and call 48-60.
   - 90 s: 0-18, 18-31, 31-72, 72-90. One metaphor from the viewer's world. [loc] explainer-video
5. UGC of 15-30 s, 9:16, <= 55 words.
   - 0-3 a person speaks to the camera, close up, a hook from pain or the result.
   - 3-8 the problem in their words. 8-20 the solution, a view of the product screen. 20-25 the result.
   - 25-30 the call. The text is written for speech, with no "advertising" words. [sec] ugcroster.com/blog/ugc-script-structure; trackbee.io
   - By the owner's edit the heroine is in frame only in the hook, then a voice-over. [loc]
6. An announcement, a launch of 15-30 s.
   - 0-3 the name and the main benefit in one phrase, the product on screen.
   - 3-15 two or three showings of 4-6 s, each about one benefit. The last 3-5 s: where to get it, the date, the call. [own]
7. A motion spot without voice-over of 10-20 s. The meaning is carried by captions and sound.
   - A caption of 1-3 words, hold >= 0.83 s, no faster than 20 characters/s. A cut on a fraction of the beat: at 120 BPM a beat is 0.5 s, a bar 2 s.
   - 4-6 scenes of 2-4 s. A bright first frame, the end card 2-3 s. Music and SFX are mandatory, the sound is written into every line. [loc] the editor's principles; [own]

8. A bumper of 6 s (YouTube, non-skippable pre-roll). One thought, one picture, the brand from the first second; voice-over <= 12 words or
   only a caption. Not a squeezed 15-second one: its own move "one scene, one twist". [off] ABCD; [own]
9. A YouTube video and tutorial of 2-10 min, 16:9.
   - 0-15 s: the promise of the result and a piece of that result; no intros and no "subscribe".
   - Then chapters of 30-90 s, each with its own mini-goal and a heading caption; a change of shot or graphics every 5-10 s.
   - Tutorial: the result shown at the start, then the steps in the order of action, every step is an action on screen and one phrase.
   - A talking head: cutaways to a screen or graphics every 10-20 s. The voice-over is the same 2.3 words/s. The call is at the end
     in one phrase. [own]
10. Adaptation 16:9 -> 9:16, 1:1, 4:5. The script is the same, the framing changes: the main thing in the center vertically, captions above the
    bottom 20% (the platform's interface), the interface in bigger scale: pieces instead of windows. In the brief you write only the differences.
    [off] Meta Safe Zone; [loc] the editor's principles
